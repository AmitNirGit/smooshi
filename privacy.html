Smooshi – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Smooshi is a game for children. We built it so that it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, send to a server, or share any personal information or usage data.

On your device

Game progress (squishies found, stations reached, and settings such as sound on or off) is saved only on your device. It never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

No ads, no purchases, no accounts

The game has no advertising, no in-app purchases, no sign-in, no chat and no links to other websites or apps.

No network

The game works fully offline and makes no internet requests.

Third parties

We use no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Children

Because we collect no information, we collect none from children under 13 (COPPA) or from anyone else.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if ads are added), we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before the change ships.
